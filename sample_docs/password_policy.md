# Password Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines requirements for the creation and management of passwords to ensure the security of corporate systems and data.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems.

## 3. Password Requirements

### 3.1 Minimum Length
- Passwords must be at least **12 characters** in length.

### 3.2 Complexity
Passwords must include at least three of the following:
- Uppercase letters (A-Z)
- Lowercase letters (a-z)
- Numbers (0-9)
- Special characters (e.g., !, @, #, $)

### 3.3 Prohibited Passwords
- Passwords must not contain:
  - Username or parts of the user’s full name
  - Common dictionary words
  - Previously used passwords (last 10)

## 4. Password Rotation
- Passwords must be changed every **90 days**.
- Service accounts must rotate credentials at least every **180 days**.

## 5. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- MFA is required for:
  - Remote access
  - Privileged accounts
  - Access to sensitive systems

## 6. Storage and Transmission
- Passwords must never be stored in plain text.
- Passwords must not be shared via email, chat, or ticketing systems.

## 7. Account Lockout
- Accounts will be locked after **5 failed login attempts**.
- Lockout duration: **15 minutes**.

## 8. Responsibilities
Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their passwords.

## 9. Exceptions
Any exceptions must be approved by the Information Security team.

## 10. Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
