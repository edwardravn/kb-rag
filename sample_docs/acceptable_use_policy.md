# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines acceptable use of company systems, networks, and data.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties using company IT resources.

## 3. Acceptable Use
Users are permitted to:
- Access systems required for their job responsibilities
- Use email and collaboration tools for business purposes
- Access the internet for work-related activities

## 4. Prohibited Activities
Users must not:
- Install unauthorized software
- Access or distribute illegal content
- Attempt to bypass security controls
- Use company systems for personal financial gain
- Share credentials with others

## 5. Internet Usage
- Limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work responsibilities
- Access to high-risk websites may be restricted

## 6. Monitoring
- All activity on company systems may be monitored and logged
- Users should have no expectation of privacy

## 7. Data Handling
- Sensitive data must not be stored on personal devices
- Data must be classified and handled according to company standards

## 8. Remote Work
- Only approved devices may be used
- VPN must be used when accessing company systems remotely

## 9. Enforcement
Violations may result in:
- Revocation of access
- Disciplinary action
- Legal action if applicable
